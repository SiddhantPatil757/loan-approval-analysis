powerbi presention 

“I developed a Loan Approval and Credit Risk dashboard to analyze factors influencing loan decisions and identify high-risk applicants.

From the overall analysis, I observed that around 62% of loan applications are approved, while about 15% are rejected and 22% remain pending, indicating a relatively healthy approval rate but some operational delays.

From a risk perspective, low-risk applicants have significantly higher approval rates, whereas high-risk customers are more likely to be rejected. This highlights that CIBIL score plays a critical role in loan approval decisions.

Financial analysis shows that applicants with higher debt-to-income (DTI) and EMI-to-income ratios are more likely to be rejected, indicating that repayment capacity is a key factor in credit evaluation.

Additionally, customers with a history of previous loan defaults show a much higher rejection rate, making past behavior an important risk indicator.

The rejection analysis further reveals that the top reasons for rejection are low CIBIL score, low income, and high existing EMI obligations.

Finally, geographical analysis shows variation in loan applications across states, which can help financial institutions target regions more effectively.

Overall, this dashboard helps in identifying high-risk applicants, improving loan approval strategies, and making data-driven credit decisions.”

“This dashboard can help banks improve risk assessment and optimize loan approval strategies.”
